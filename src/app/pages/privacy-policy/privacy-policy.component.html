Privacy Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

What quizmastr is

quizmastr is a free trivia quiz game. You can play alone, or host or join a quiz with friends in real time. There's no account system - you just type in a display name when you start playing.

What information we collect

The only information quizmastr itself asks for is the display name you type in when hosting, joining, or starting a solo game. That name, along with your score and answers for that game, exists only in memory for the lifetime of that quiz session - it's never written to a database, and it's gone as soon as the quiz ends or the server restarts. We don't require or collect email addresses, passwords, or any other personal account information, because there's no account to create.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server access logs (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) for security and operational purposes, the same way most websites do. We don't use this information to identify individual players.

Cookies and advertising

quizmastr uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and opt out of personalized advertising, at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and adssettings.google.com. quizmastr doesn't set any advertising or tracking cookies of its own.

Third-party content

Trivia questions are sourced from Open Trivia DB, a free, community-maintained trivia question database. No personal information is sent to Open Trivia DB as part of fetching questions.

Children's privacy

quizmastr is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
